Sandboardghini

A 2.5-DOF sand drawing robot that transforms digital patterns into physical art using a magnetic end effector and precision motion system.

General Description

Sandboardghini is a box filled with sand, designed to create precise sand drawings using a steel ball resting on the surface. Underneath the box, a rail-mounted end effector moves in two dimensions (X and Y) powered by stepper motors. A servo-driven mechanism raises or lowers a magnet below the box, which engages or disengages the steel ball above. When the magnet is raised, the magnetic force is strong enough to “capture” the ball, allowing the end effector to drag it through the sand to draw shapes.

Motion System

The system uses high-precision stepper motors on both X and Y axes. The Y-axis carriage, which moves along the upper rail, is itself moved by two synchronized X-axis rails. For vertical motion, a high-torque F90R servo controls the magnet lift mechanism, providing the crucial “on/off” control of the magnetic field without disturbing the overall motion.

End Effector Design

The end effector was custom-designed to house the magnet and lift mechanism. It features a servo-actuated linkage that pushes the magnet holder up and down a vertical guide channel. The main body was 3D printed to achieve a complex geometry, while the 2D linkage components were laser cut for precision and light weight.

Manufacturing and Assembly

The system integrates several fabrication methods: 3D printing was used for mounts, carriages, and motor brackets; laser cutting was used for flat linkage parts; and standard aluminum 80/20 extrusion served as the structural frame, with minor drilling performed for assembly. This hybrid approach balanced strength, precision, and ease of assembly.

Code and Control

The control system is powered by a Maker Base board, which synchronizes the stepper motors and servo. The software interprets uploaded SVG or STL files and generates G-code, much like a 3D printer slicer. This allows the system to replicate virtually any shape or pattern with fine detail, all through an intuitive upload and execution process.

Results

Sandboardghini successfully draws arbitrary shapes in sand with remarkable precision. The system can recreate complex designs by simply uploading an SVG or STL file, which is then processed and sliced to generate the required motion paths. It combines engineering precision with artistic expression, transforming digital input into tactile output.
